# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
The Engineering Lead owns the technical direction of a delivery effort, ensuring the implementation is sound, maintainable, and aligned with architecture standards. They guide and mentor developers while keeping delivery on track.

### Responsibilities
- Technical ownership of implementation, architecture decisions, and code quality
- Mentor the engineering team and unblock technical issues
- Review high-risk changes and enforce engineering standards
- Balance technical debt against delivery commitments

### Goals
- Ship well-architected, maintainable solutions
- Grow the technical capability of the team
- Keep technical risk visible and managed

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review of critical changes
- Coordination with QA and SRE for release readiness

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers and Project Managers to translate requirements into technical scope
- Coordinates with QA and SRE for release readiness

---

## Delivery Lead (Scrum Master)

### Role Summary
The Delivery Lead facilitates the team's delivery process, protecting focus, removing impediments, and maintaining a healthy, predictable cadence.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Remove process blockers and shield the team from disruption
- Track and improve delivery cadence and team health

### Goals
- Maintain a sustainable, predictable delivery rhythm
- Continuously improve team process through retrospectives
- Increase transparency of progress and blockers

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Impediment logs and burndown updates

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Project Managers for scheduling
- Works with Developers and QA to clear impediments
- Aligns with stakeholders on meeting logistics

---

## Technical Program Manager (TPM)

### Role Summary
The Technical Program Manager drives cross-team planning and coordination for complex, multi-team initiatives, keeping dependencies and technical risks visible and managed.

### Responsibilities
- Own cross-team planning, roadmap dependencies, and technical risk mitigation across multiple engineering teams
- Coordinate milestones and integration points between teams
- Surface and drive resolution of cross-cutting risks

### Goals
- Deliver complex, multi-team programs predictably
- Reduce cross-team dependency friction
- Keep technical risk visible at the program level

### Typical Communication
- Program status reports and dependency reviews
- Cross-team syncs and integration planning

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers, Engineering Leads, and Project Managers to align scope, schedule, and delivery across teams

---

## Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)

### Role Summary
The SRE focuses on the reliability, observability, and operability of the product in production, partnering with engineering to make releases safe and recoverable.

### Responsibilities
- Define and monitor service-level objectives (SLOs) and alerting
- Own incident response readiness and rollback procedures
- Improve observability, automation, and operational tooling

### Goals
- Maximize reliability and minimize mean time to recovery
- Make releases safe, observable, and reversible
- Reduce operational toil through automation

### Typical Communication
- Incident reviews and postmortems
- Pre-release reliability checks and runbooks

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Engineering Leads and Developers on release readiness
- Coordinates with Project Managers during incidents and escalations
